## Other ways to help

We welcome all your ideas about new or existing features: [open an issue](https://github.com/autoNumeric/autoNumeric/issues/new) or [create a pull request](https://github.com/autoNumeric/autoNumeric/compare) on GitHub. Any help is *highly* appreciated.

You can also come say hi on our [chat](https://gitter.im/autoNumeric/autoNumeric). We don't bite!
